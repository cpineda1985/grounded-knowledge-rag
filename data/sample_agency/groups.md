# Group Bookings

> Fictional policy for Puerto Azul Travel, a made-up agency used as demo data.

## What counts as a group

A group booking is 10 or more travelers on the same itinerary and dates. Smaller parties are booked as individual reservations.

## Group deposit

Groups pay a deposit of USD 150 per traveler to hold space. The deposit is due within 7 days of signing the group contract.

## Rooming list

The group organizer must send the final rooming list, with every traveler's full name as shown on their passport, no later than 45 days before departure.

## Group discounts

Groups of 20 or more travelers receive one free place for the organizer, which is applied to the lowest-priced room category.

## Cancellations inside a group

If individual travelers cancel, the group price may be recalculated when the group falls below 10 travelers.
