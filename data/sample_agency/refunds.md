# Refunds

> Fictional policy for Puerto Azul Travel, a made-up agency used as demo data.

## Refund timelines

Refunds for agency fees are processed within 10 business days. Refunds that depend on a supplier, such as an airline or hotel, are paid to the client only after the supplier pays the agency, which can take 30 to 90 days.

## Refund method

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Cash payments are refunded by bank transfer to an account in the client's name. The agency never refunds to a third party's account.

## Non-refundable items

Travel insurance premiums, visa processing fees paid to third parties, and non-refundable hotel rates are not refundable.

## Supplier credits

When a supplier offers a credit instead of a refund, the advisor must explain to the client in writing how long the credit is valid and any conditions for using it. The client chooses whether to accept the credit or wait for a refund when the supplier allows both.
