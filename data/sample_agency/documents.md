# Travel Documents

> Fictional policy for Puerto Azul Travel, a made-up agency used as demo data.

## Agency role

Entry requirements, visas, and passport rules are set by each country and can change. The agency does not give legal or immigration advice. Advisors must tell clients to confirm requirements with the official embassy or consulate of the destination before paying for the trip.

## Passport reminder

At the time of booking, advisors remind clients to check the expiration date of their passport, because many destinations and airlines refuse travelers whose passport expires soon.

## Minors traveling

When a minor travels with only one parent or with other adults, the advisor must remind the family to check which permits or notarized authorizations are required, and to carry them on the trip.

## Visa processing support

The agency can help schedule visa appointments and fill out forms for a fee of USD 35 per application. The agency cannot guarantee that a visa will be approved.
