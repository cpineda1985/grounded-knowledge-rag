# Payments and Installments

> Fictional policy for Puerto Azul Travel, a made-up agency used as demo data.

## Deposit

Vacation packages require a 30% deposit to confirm the booking. The deposit must be paid within 48 hours of the quote being accepted, or the held space is released.

## Installment plans

After the deposit, clients may pay the balance in up to 4 monthly installments. The full balance must be paid no later than 30 days before departure.

Bookings made less than 30 days before departure must be paid in full at the time of booking.

## Accepted payment methods

The agency accepts cash at any branch, bank transfer, and credit or debit card. Card payments above USD 2,000 require the cardholder to sign an authorization form.

## Split payments

A single sale can be paid with more than one method, for example part in cash and part by card. Each payment is recorded separately, and the remaining balance updates automatically after every payment.

## Late payments

If an installment is more than 7 days late, the advisor contacts the client. If the balance is not paid by the 30-day deadline, the booking is cancelled under the cancellation policy.
