# Baggage Guidance

> Fictional policy for Puerto Azul Travel, a made-up agency used as demo data.

## Who sets baggage rules

Baggage allowances are set by each airline and depend on the fare type. The agency does not set or guarantee baggage allowances. Advisors must copy the allowance shown in the airline reservation into the client's itinerary.

## Extra baggage

Extra bags can be added through the agency for a service fee of USD 10 per booking, plus the airline's price. It is usually cheaper to add bags before travel than at the airport.

## Lost or delayed baggage

If baggage is lost or delayed, the client must file a report with the airline at the airport before leaving the arrivals area. The advisor can then help follow up with the airline using the report number.
