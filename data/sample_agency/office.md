# Office Hours, Branches and Roles

> Fictional policy for Puerto Azul Travel, a made-up agency used as demo data.

## Office hours

Branches are open Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00, and Saturday from 9:00 to 13:00. Branches are closed on Sundays and national holidays. The 24/7 emergency line is for clients who are already traveling.

## Branches

Puerto Azul Travel has two fictional branches: Centro and Bahía. Each branch has its own supervisor and its own invoice numbering.

## Roles

- Agent (advisor): creates quotes and sales, and sees only their own sales.
- Supervisor: sees every sale in their branch, approves fee waivers and credit notes, and records commission splits.
- Administrator: manages users, branches, and agency-wide settings, and sees reports for all branches.

## Contact channels

Clients can reach their advisor by phone, email, or messaging during office hours. Written requests, such as cancellations, must be sent by email.
