# Supplier Issues and Escalations

> Fictional policy for Puerto Azul Travel, a made-up agency used as demo data.

## Problems during a trip

Clients who have a problem during their trip, such as a missing hotel reservation or a missed transfer, call the agency's 24/7 emergency line. The on-call advisor must answer within 15 minutes.

## Escalation levels

1. The advisor contacts the supplier and tries to solve the issue.
2. If it is not solved within 2 hours, the advisor escalates to the branch supervisor.
3. If the issue affects a group or costs more than USD 500 to fix, the supervisor informs the agency director.

## Alternative arrangements

If a hotel cannot honor a confirmed reservation, the advisor books an equivalent or better hotel and the agency covers the price difference, then claims it back from the original supplier.

## Recording incidents

Every incident is recorded in the sale with what happened, what was done, and the final cost, so the agency can claim it from the supplier.
