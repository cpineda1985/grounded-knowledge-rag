# Advisor Commissions

> Fictional policy for Puerto Azul Travel, a made-up agency used as demo data.

## Commission rates

Advisors earn commission on the agency's margin, not on the full sale price:

- Vacation packages: 12% of the agency margin.
- Flight-only bookings: a flat USD 5 per ticket.
- Travel insurance: 15% of the premium.

## When commissions are paid

Commissions are paid monthly, on the 15th, for sales whose travel was completed during the previous month. Commissions are not paid on bookings that are still pending travel.

## Cancellations and clawback

If a sale is cancelled after its commission was paid, the commission is deducted from the advisor's next payment.

## Shared sales

When two advisors work on the same sale, the commission is split 50/50 unless a supervisor records a different split in the sale before travel.
