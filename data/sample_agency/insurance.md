# Travel Insurance

> Fictional policy for Puerto Azul Travel, a made-up agency used as demo data.

## Plans offered

The agency offers two fictional plans from its insurance partner:

- Basic: medical assistance abroad and lost baggage coverage.
- Plus: everything in Basic, plus trip cancellation for covered reasons, such as a documented illness.

## When to buy

Insurance must be purchased at the same time as the trip or within 72 hours of paying the deposit. After that, the Plus plan can no longer be added.

## Filing a claim

Claims are filed directly with the insurance partner. The advisor provides the policy number and the partner's claims contact, and can help the client gather receipts and documents.

## Advisor responsibilities

Advisors must offer insurance on every international booking and record in the sale whether the client accepted or declined it.
