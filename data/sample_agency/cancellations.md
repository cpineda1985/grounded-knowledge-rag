# Cancellation Policy

> Fictional policy for Puerto Azul Travel, a made-up agency used as demo data.

## Vacation packages

Clients may cancel a vacation package at any time. Charges depend on how many days before departure the cancellation is received in writing:

- 45 days or more: the 30% deposit is kept as a cancellation fee; the rest is refunded.
- 15 to 44 days: 50% of the package price is charged.
- 14 days or fewer: 100% of the package price is charged.

Cancellations must be sent by email to the assigned advisor. Phone calls alone do not count as a written cancellation.

## Flight-only bookings

For flight-only bookings, the airline fare rules apply. Puerto Azul Travel adds a service fee of USD 25 per ticket for processing any cancellation, even when the fare itself is refundable.

## Hotel-only bookings

Hotel-only bookings follow the cancellation deadline shown on the client's confirmation. After that deadline, the hotel usually charges the first night. Non-refundable rates cannot be cancelled for a refund.

## Group bookings

Group cancellations follow the group contract. See the group bookings policy for deposit and rooming list deadlines.

## Force majeure

If a trip cannot happen because of a hurricane, a border closure, or an official travel ban at the destination, the agency waives its own service fees. Supplier charges still apply, but advisors must request supplier credits or refunds on the client's behalf.
