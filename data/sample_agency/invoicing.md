# Invoices and Receipts

> Fictional policy for Puerto Azul Travel, a made-up agency used as demo data.

## Receipts for each payment

Every payment, including each installment, gets an internal receipt with the amount, method, date, and remaining balance.

## Invoices

An invoice is issued when the sale is fully paid. Invoices use sequential numbering per branch, and numbers are never reused or skipped.

## Correcting an invoice

An issued invoice is never edited or deleted. To correct it, the advisor issues a credit note that references the original invoice, and then issues a new invoice with the correct data. Credit notes require supervisor approval.

## Branch details

Each branch prints its own name, tax number, and logo on receipts and invoices.
